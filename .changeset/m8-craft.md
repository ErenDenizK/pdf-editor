M8 craft: a document now opens in Read, where nothing on the page can change, and Edit (keys
1 and 2, with Arrange on 3 and Compare on 4); Home is its own view of the open files (0) and
remembers recently opened files on this device, reopening them where the browser allows,
with "Clear recents" always at hand. In Edit the tool bar has five groups (Select, Write,
Text, Fill & sign, Redact), a selected text offers its markups and a comment, and a
right-click on a page offers rotate, delete, crop and Arrange. Double-click a paragraph, or
use Edit text, and type: the paragraph rewraps in the file's own font, a character the font
lacks is set in a named bundled font, and every edit is checked before it is kept. The pen
keeps up with the hand: a constant width with a mouse, no change of shape when you lift, and
a finished stroke shows at once instead of waiting for the page to redraw. One Highlighter
replaces the two: drawn along text it becomes a highlight, elsewhere a yellow mark that
keeps black text black. The lasso takes every kind of annotation and can resize and rotate
them together, the eraser can cut part of a stroke, and holding still while drawing makes a
straight line. One clean palette of eight inks and four highlighter tints, each checked for
contrast. Keyboard users reach one target per paragraph, every new surface passes the
accessibility checks in English and Turkish, and a "Glass panels" setting (off by default)
and "Reduce transparency" are in the Document menu under Appearance.
